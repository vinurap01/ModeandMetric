Privacy notice

What the Mode & Metric website collects, why, and who else handles it.

Last updated 24 October 2026

What we collect

The only personal details this site asks for are in the free store review form:

  • Your email address, so we can send you the review.
  • Your store's web address, so we know which store to review.

There are no accounts, logins or other forms on the site.

How we use it

We use your email address and store address to review your store, send you the video walkthrough, and follow up with you about it.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives the form when you send it and passes it on to us. Formspree handles that data under its own privacy policy, and may store it outside Australia.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this site. Like most hosts, it records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, approximate location and browser type, to deliver and secure the site. Vercel may process this outside Australia, including in the United States. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address and store address for 12 months after we last contact you, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what details we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete them, by emailing vin@convertifyco.com.
