Common questions

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with the free review. You'll see what we'd fix first, why we think it matters, and what we'd recommend doing next — with no obligation.

If you want us to implement the fixes, we'll scope the work and give you one fixed price before anything starts. You decide whether to go ahead.

How much will my store cost to fix?

It depends on what we find. Conversion improvement projects start from A$2,500, and we scope the work against your store before anything begins.

You'll know exactly what we'd fix and what it costs before you commit. The agreed price doesn't change once work starts.

Will this definitely work for me?

No, and we'd never promise that. CRO isn't something anyone can guarantee.

What we can do is identify the highest-confidence issues we see, explain why we think they're hurting sales, and let you decide whether they're worth fixing before you spend anything.

That's why every project starts with the free review.

How is this different from a CRO retainer?

Most CRO retainers are ongoing monthly engagements.

Mode & Metric is one-off and focused: we find the highest-impact issues hurting conversions, quote a fixed price, and make the changes.

No retainer to start. We scope the work, agree the price, make the changes, and finish the project.

Do you redesign the whole store?

No. Most stores don't need a full redesign.

We focus on the specific issues hurting conversions, usually things like mobile UX, CTA visibility, trust positioning, product page structure, clarity, and site friction.

The goal is to improve how the store converts, not completely change how it looks.

Why do you only work with fashion stores?

Fashion has its own conversion problems: fit uncertainty, sizing, product confidence, imagery, returns, delivery expectations and how products are discovered.

By working only with fashion stores, we get better at recognising those problems and knowing what to look for, instead of applying the same CRO checklist to every business.

Can I still edit my store after?

Yes. We don't lock anything or change your setup. You can keep editing your store like normal.
